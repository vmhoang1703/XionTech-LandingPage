How our team is putting agentic AI to work every day

At our “Thực Chiến AI với Agentic Workflow” workshop, team members showed how they use AI tools in their everyday work, then stayed on to celebrate together.

Applying AI not only to software development but also to everyday work activities is becoming increasingly important for improving productivity and efficiency. That idea shaped the internal workshop we held on July 31.

Learning from the people doing the work

The “Thực Chiến AI với Agentic Workflow” workshop was led by our own team members. Colleagues shared practical experiences from their day-to-day roles and demonstrated how they leverage AI tools to take on parts of their daily work.

Because the examples came from real work, the session quickly became an exchange of ideas. Team members learned from one another and explored new ways to integrate AI into their own workflows, so they can work smarter and more effectively.

Beyond the workshop

To conclude the event, we enjoyed a team-building gathering where conversations extended beyond work. It was a valuable time to connect, share personal experiences and strengthen relationships across the team.

Moments like this reinforce the culture that makes newXion feel not just like a workplace, but like a family, and keep learning an everyday habit rather than a one-off event.
