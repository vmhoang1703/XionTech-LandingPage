Rules that keep it readable

Amber textOn light grounds amber words use --amber-text; bright amber only fills buttons and shapes.
Amber tintOn navy, accents and highlighted words use --amber-tint.
Navy on amberText on amber is always navy; one amber band per page at most.

Every piece of visible text is checked against its real rendered background: WCAG AA (4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text).

03 / Foundation

Surfaces and backgrounds

Sections take a surface, and components read scoped tokens (--heading, --text, --muted, --border), so the same card works on any of them.

.s-paperWhite paper, the default.
.s-mistMist blue tint for quiet bands.
.s-navyInk to navy, white text.
.s-amberAmber band, navy text, one per page.

Flowing page backgrounds

With JavaScript, one gradient is drawn down the whole page (.flow-bg, by palette) and sections switch text colour by phase. The change from dark to light always falls in the space between sections, never under text.

Home · palette "home"Hero navy into blue, light blue, white and the amber contact form, with a fine grain so the fade shows no bands.
Services · palette "mesh"Light blue around the navy and light blue panels, turning white at the end, with white and amber glows.
Careers · palette "pastel"Light blue, then white, then warm amber towards the footer.
Do Let colours melt into one another; glows are soft radial washes in white, amber or light blue.
Don't Put hard colour edges between sections, show stepped bands, or place mid-tones under text.

10 / Feel

Motion and voice

Motion explains order and arrival, then gets out of the way. Every animation stops with the system's reduced-motion setting.

RevealSections fade up 26px, heading first, then content. .9s · --ease cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)
UnfoldNews cards open from the top like a menu, one after another across a row. 1.25s · 260ms apart
SpringServices metric cards rise with a small overshoot; service cards tilt in and settle. 1.1s · cubic-bezier(.34,1.45,.5,1)

Voice

Do Write plain, warm English with a Vietnamese version of every visible line (data-vi, data-vi-html, data-vi-aria); use real content, or mark it [Placeholder].
Don't Invent figures, testimonials or partners; use lorem ipsum; or write long sentences where a short one will do.
