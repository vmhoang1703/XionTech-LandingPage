XionTechs — Color Themes v1

Same brand colors, five different proportions. Pick one, or mix: e.g. 'hero from 5, cards from 2'.

Reference: white #FFFFFF on amber #FFC000 = 1.64:1 (FAIL), so amber always carries navy text: #163663 on #FFC000 = 7.34:1. Amber on white = 1.64:1, so amber is never used as text or as a focus ring on white surfaces; light surfaces use a navy focus ring.
